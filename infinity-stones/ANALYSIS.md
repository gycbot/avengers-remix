### Cut and optical pipeline
Loose screen props are not six identical jewelry diamonds; current elongated oval brilliants are deliberate readable set-form stylization from a real CC BY4.0 faceted mesh (96triangles, not a high-poly scan). Flat crown/table/pavilion normals catch the realHDR. The unchanged approved shader performs actual GPU BVH/TIR four-bounce RGB dispersion, not an inset picture or convex-plane approximation.

Original environment is EXR→PMREM, **not KTX2**; KTX2 supplies compressed normals, roughness/noise and lookup textures. Original half-float/MRT/info/frost, six-level bloom, LUT and SMAA remain. Frost is a controlled interactive energy reveal, not an opaque icy coat over every polished gem. Inner density glow is subtle; white specular/refraction are not replaced by a full-surface emissive bulb. Refraction/bloom is not global caustic projection/path tracing.
