### Finite thickness / final absorption correction
Private native QA found a real numerical problem in the upstream thickness disc shader: the circle discard and square-root expression used differently arranged arithmetic, allowing tiny negative square-root inputs at fragment edges. One NaN then contaminated additive thickness and the Gaussian filter, defeating absorption. The standard `sqrt(max(0.,1.-dot(xy,xy)))` boundary guard fixes it without changing the particle kernel or solver. A/B center thickness went from NaN to27.3916 (GL0); no texture readback ships. Beer-Lambert coefficient .065 now uses the measured finite thickness, instead of tuning against invalid data. Reflections and refracted photographic environment remain visible through blue thickness absorption, not an opaque tint overlay. `_qa/pbf-thickness-inspect.json` and final full native captures document this. The raw upstream source remains archived unchanged.
